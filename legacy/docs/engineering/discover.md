# discover

`/discover` maps unclear work before it becomes an issue, spec, or implementation plan.

It is for foggy ideas with unknowns, research needs, prototype candidates, or multiple possible paths.

## Use When

- Product direction is unclear.
- Several decisions block implementation.
- The idea may need research or a prototype first.
- The work probably needs multiple issues.

## Output

A discovery map with goal, known facts, unknowns, decisions needed, research needed, prototype needed, and proposed issues.

## Important Rule

Do not pretend unknowns are decisions. Keep uncertainty visible.
