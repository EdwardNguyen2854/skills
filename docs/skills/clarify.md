# Clarify outcomes

`/clarify`

Investigates technical uncertainty and asks product questions in everyday language with practical consequences and recommendations.

## Use when

- The idea is broad or ambiguous.
- A technical choice changes the user experience.
- The owner is unavailable and scoped judgment mode can safely handle assumptions.

## Outcome

Verified facts, owner answers, recorded assumptions and a clear indication of whether planning can proceed.

## Key safeguard

It does not invent audience or requirements, ask the owner to choose engineering internals, or treat silence as permission.
