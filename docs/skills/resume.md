# Resume saved work

`/resume`

Reconciles compact local records with actual files, branches, PRs, workers and evidence, then rebuilds or reuses native tasks.

## Use when

- Continuing in a new session.
- Returning after an interruption.
- Saved progress may be stale or a worker may still be running.

## Outcome

A plain-language account of what is actually complete, blocked or uncertain, followed by the authorized next action.

## Key safeguard

It does not trust checkboxes, inherit session permission, revive a revoked grant from an old checkpoint, or overwrite unexpected work.
